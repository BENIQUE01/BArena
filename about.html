Built in Kenya for every league.

Barena Hub is a tournament platform that starts on local grounds and is made to reach the world.

Too many good tournaments still run on paper. Draws get lost, scores get argued over, and players never build a record of how well they have played.

Barena Hub gives organizers one app for registration, brackets and live scoring, and gives players a rating and a Sport Passport ID that stay with them from county grounds to international events.

We are growing sport by sport and county by county. If you run a league, a club or a school event, talk to us.
