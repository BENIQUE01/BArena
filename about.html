Frequently asked questions

How do I register for a tournament? +
Visit the Tournaments page, find the event you want to enter, and click Register. You'll select your category, fill in your details, and receive payment instructions. Your slot is secured once payment is confirmed.
What sports does BArena support? +
BArena is designed to handle 250+ sports. The current pilot focuses on Banana Smash in Kiambu County, but the platform can onboard any sport that uses brackets, round-robins, or league formats.
How do I confirm my payment? +
After registering, send your M-Pesa payment code to the number provided. An admin will verify and flip your status to Confirmed — you'll see a green ✓ next to your name on the tournament page.
Can I bring BArena to my county? +
Yes — use the contact form above and select "Host a tournament." We'll walk you through setting up your county edition on the same platform, with the same real-time features as the Kiambu pilot.
Is BArena free for players? +
The BArena app and website are free to use. Tournaments may charge an entry fee set by the organiser — this is always displayed on the tournament page before you register.
