Built in Kenya for every league.

Barena Hub is a tournament platform that starts on local grounds and is made to reach the world.

Our story

Too many good tournaments still run on paper.

Draws get lost, scores get argued over, and players never build a record of how well they have played.

Barena Hub gives organizers one app for registration, brackets and live scoring, and gives every player a rating and a Sport Passport ID that stay with them from county grounds to international events.

What we believe

Three ideas behind the app.

Every match counts

A score, a rating and a place in a player’s story. Nothing should be lost on a scrap of paper.

Open to every sport

From table tennis to football, every game deserves proper scoring and a fair draw.

Built for the grounds

It has to work where tournaments really happen, on a phone, in the sun, with a crowd.

Our mission

Give every player a story worth keeping.

We are growing sport by sport and county by county. If you run a league, a club or a school event, we would love to hear from you.
