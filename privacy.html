1. Who we are

BArena ("BArena", "we", "us") operates the BArena mobile app and the website at benique01.github.io/BArena, a platform for organizing and following sports tournaments — from single-club events up to county, national, and continental competitions.

This policy explains what information we collect through the app and website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies wherever BArena is available, including if you download it via the Google Play Store, APKPure, or another distribution channel.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in three ways: what you give us directly, what's generated as you use the app, and what's created about matches and tournaments you're part of.

Account information

  • Full name, username, and email address
  • Phone number (optional, depending on how you sign up)
  • Profile photo, short bio, and nationality
  • Account type (e.g. player, organizer, club admin, scorer) and any club or institution you're affiliated with
  • Subscription plan and status

Player & athlete information

If you (or your child) compete in tournaments run through BArena, we also hold:

  • Date of birth (used to place players in the correct age category)
  • Sport, category, and club/institution affiliation
  • Match results, scores, ELO-style ratings, medals, and sportsmanship records
  • For players under 18: a parent or guardian's name, phone number, and email, collected as part of our parental consent process

Usage & device information

  • Login sessions and device identifiers, used to keep your account secure and to prevent one account being logged into on multiple devices at once
  • App activity timestamps (e.g. when you were last active)
  • Offline actions queued on your device before they sync to our servers (such as scores entered while offline)
  • Basic technical data such as app version and platform (Android/iOS), for crash-fixing and support

Payment & subscription data

If you subscribe to a paid plan, purchases are processed by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store and managed through RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. BArena does not receive or store your card details — we receive confirmation of your subscription status only.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, we use a device push token to send you match updates, tournament announcements, or account-related alerts. You can turn these off at any time in Settings.

App permissions

BArena asks for the following device permissions, only for the features described:

  • Photo library: to let you choose a profile picture. We only access the photo you select — we don't browse your library otherwise.
  • Notifications: to send match and tournament updates, if you've turned this on in Settings.

You can review or revoke any of these permissions at any time from your device's app settings.

We do not collect precise GPS location. If a future version of the app requests location access (for example, to show nearby tournaments), that will always be a separate, optional permission you control from your device settings.

3. Children's data & parental consent

BArena is used by youth sports leagues, so we knowingly collect limited information about players under 18. We do this only with a parent or guardian's consent, collected through our parental consent form at the time of registration.

  • A parent or guardian provides consent before a minor's player profile is created.
  • We collect the minor's name, date of birth, sport/category information, and match results — the minimum needed to run age-appropriate competition.
  • We collect a parent or guardian's name, phone number, and email so we can reach them, not the child directly, about their account.
  • A parent or guardian can request to review, correct, or delete their child's information at any time by emailing us at barenahub@gmail.com.

We do not knowingly collect information directly from a child without a parent or guardian's involvement in the registration process. If you believe a minor's data was submitted without appropriate consent, contact us and we will act on it promptly.

4. How we use information

  • To create and run your account, and to place players in the correct tournament categories
  • To operate tournaments — brackets, live scoring, standings, and ratings
  • To keep your account secure, including detecting when an account is active on more than one device
  • To sync data you enter offline once your device reconnects, and to back up your account so it survives a phone loss or reinstall
  • To send you notifications you've opted into
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests
  • To meet legal obligations, such as responding to a verified data request

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that make BArena work, and only as needed for them to provide that service:

Other players, coaches, and the public may see information you choose to make visible in a tournament context — for example, your name, club, and match results on a public bracket or leaderboard, since that's the nature of a tournament platform. Contact details and a minor's date of birth are never shown publicly.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of BArena, our users, or the public.

6. Storage, backups & security

Your data is stored with Supabase and protected by access controls that restrict who can read or modify it. Passwords are never stored in plain text. Sensitive actions — like deleting your account — require you to re-enter your password, so that having a signed-in device alone isn't enough to delete an account.

BArena also supports offline use: data you create without a connection is stored on your device and uploaded once you're back online. If you enable cloud backup, a copy of your account's data is stored so it can be restored on a new device.

7. How long we keep data

We keep account and player data for as long as your account is active. Match results, scores, and ratings that involve other players are kept even after you delete your account, but with your personal details removed — this is because other players' match history and tournament records need to stay accurate and intact. See "Deleting your account" below for exactly what is and isn't removed.

8. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information (most fields can be edited directly in the app)
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent for a minor's data, as a parent or guardian
  • Opt out of notifications at any time in Settings

To exercise any of these rights, email barenahub@gmail.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time, and may need to verify your identity first.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your own account at any time from Settings → Account Management → Delete Account. You'll be asked to confirm your password before anything is deleted, to make sure it's really you. When you do:

  • Your login is permanently removed and you're immediately signed out.
  • Your name, email, phone, avatar, bio, and other personal details are erased.
  • Match history you were part of is kept, but anonymized (shown as "Deleted User"), so other players' tournament records stay accurate.

This action cannot be undone. If you'd prefer we delete a minor's account on a parent's behalf instead of doing it in-app, email us and we'll handle it directly.

10. International users

BArena is based in Kenya, and our Kiambu County pilot is our current primary user base, though the app is distributed more broadly, including via APKPure. Wherever you access BArena from, your information may be processed in a different country than the one you live in, by our service providers listed above. We take steps to keep your data secure regardless of where it's processed.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as BArena's features change. If we make a material change, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Continuing to use BArena after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data? Email us at barenahub@gmail.com.
