About KITTA
Who We Are

The Kiambu Table Tennis Association (KITTA) is the organizing body for competitive table tennis in Kiambu County — bringing together clubs, schools, and academies under one banner.

The Banana Smash Series

An open inter-club tournament series running multiple seasons per year. Any registered player from any club can enter — all skill levels welcome, with category-based competition and real cash prizes.

Powered by BArena

All KITTA tournaments are managed and scored live using the BArena app — real-time brackets, live scoring, and official results published instantly.

Venue

Home base at ACK Emmanuel Church, Karuri Parish — a central venue accessible to clubs across Kiambu County with dedicated table tennis facilities.
