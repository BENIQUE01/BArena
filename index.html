Questions, answered.

Is Barena Hub free?

Yes. The Android app is free to download.

Which sports does it support?

Football, table tennis, pool, darts, chess, basketball and more, each with its own scoring rules.

Do spectators need an account?

No. Open a tournament’s live page or scan its QR code and follow along.

Can I run a big event with many categories?

Yes. Create categories, choose single or double elimination, round-robin or groups, and manage scorers and referees.

Is it on iPhone or Google Play?

The app is on Android today. News about the Google Play listing and other devices is posted on the blog.
