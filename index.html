Platform Overview

What is BARENA?

A unified tournament management system designed for scale. From a church league to a national championship — BARENA handles it.

📊

Real-Time Management

Live scoreboards, instant bracket updates, and player ratings that change with every match. Transparency built in.

🏅

Player Progression

ELO ratings, sport passports, and badges that carry meaning. Athletes build a permanent record across seasons.

🎯

Flexible Formats

Single & double elimination, round-robin, group stages, and custom formats. Configure any tournament structure in seconds.

🔒

Role-Based Security

Institution, County, National, and Global admin roles. Each level controls its scope. No bottlenecks, no compromises.
