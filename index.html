Features

Everything a tournament needs, in one app.

Score live, finish on time

Tap to score from the sideline. The bracket, the table and every spectator’s phone update the moment a point is played.

  • Real-time scoreboard on every device
  • Single or double elimination, round-robin and group stages
  • Automatic podium and final standings

A rating that follows you

Every match moves your rating, and you see what is on the line before you play. Build a record that travels across tournaments, towns and seasons.

  • Ratings by sport, updated after every match
  • A Sport Passport ID that stays with you
  • Leaderboards you can search and share

Organizers get their weekends back

Open registration, take M-Pesa payments, generate the draw and hand out roles to scorers and referees. Start from your phone, no laptop needed.

  • Registration and payment tracking
  • Seeding by rating, random or manual
  • Admin roles from a single club to a whole county

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Barena Hub free?

Yes. The Android app is free to download.

Which sports does it support?

Football, table tennis, pool, darts, chess, basketball and more, each with its own scoring rules.

Do spectators need an account?

No. Open a tournament’s live page or scan its QR code and follow along.

Can I run a big event with many categories?

Yes. Create categories, choose single or double elimination, round-robin or groups, and manage scorers and referees.

Is it on iPhone or Google Play?

The app is on Android today. News about the Google Play listing and other devices is posted on the blog.
